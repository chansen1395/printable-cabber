# Buy vs build

This is a cost comparison, not a verdict. The design has not been built. The parts for it cost an estimated **$80**, or **$233** with four budget wheels, pads and a dressing stick. The cheapest new 4-plus-wheel machine is about $840. Prices checked 2026-10-22.

| Option | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Build (this design, not yet built)** | **$80 + $153 wheels and pads = $233** (+ ~$40 PETG) | 4 wheels + end disk, variable speed; estimated about 68 h of printing and an afternoon of assembly |
| VEVOR 6 in cabbing machine (import) | $840–1,090 | 6 wheels + lap, 1/4 HP, 1,800 RPM |
| CabKing 6V3 | $1,399 | 6 wheels |
| Kingsley North Cabber 6 | $1,399–1,499 | 6 wheels, discs, pump |
| Diamond Pacific Genie G2 | $2,426.50 | 6 wheels, 1/2 HP |
| Hi-Tech Slant Cabber 6 in | $549–599 | One flat lap |
| Used | Asking $1,500–2,200 | varies |

The commercial machines have stronger motors; this design has a light 775. If it performs as designed, it would cost about a quarter of the cheapest new machine, and every part would be either printable again or widely available. That is an expectation, not a measured result.
